FieldWorks Lite logo

A navy dictionary with a thumb index. The coral tab is the word you are looking up; the ล on the cover is shared with Lexbox. This page tells you which file to use where, and every file can be downloaded from it.

Which file do I need?

The logo is drawn four times, from detailed to simple. Pick the drawing by the size it will appear at on screen, and the reversed version when the ground is navy or dark.

The four drawings

Every icon below is shown at its real size in screen pixels. Smaller drawings turn the book less, pull the tabs further out and drop what can no longer be seen: the page edges below 48, the ล below 24.

Why not just shrink the big one?

Below, each icon is drawn into real pixels at 24 and 16 px, then blown up 4x so you can see them. The big drawing's thin lines, page edges and letter turn to grey mush; the small drawings are built for those pixels.

An app header at 32 px: -tiny-reversed.svg on navy, -tiny.svg on white. (The name is set in the app's own font; there is no wordmark file.)
Android at 60 px. The phone or launcher picks the shape, so the app gives Android two layers, a white background and the book (-circle.svg) at 85% of the icon, so it fills the plate without crowding the edge. The file already centres the book for a circle (on its visual weight, not its outline), so it needs no nudge. The icon looks the same in light and dark mode: Android has no dark app icon. Themed icons (Android 13 and later) swap our colours for a one-colour layer tinted to the wallpaper; the one-colour drawing stands in for it here. The navy fallback (-reversed-circle.svg on navy) is only for a store or launcher that needs a dark icon. The splash screen uses -splash.svg on navy #1f2a44: Android 12 and later show only the middle 2/3 circle of a splash icon, and the book sits inside it.
iPhone at 60 px: -appicon-square.svg, a full square with no transparency (iOS rounds the corners itself, and App Store Connect rejects an icon with an alpha channel). Since iOS 18 people can switch the home screen to dark or tinted; the app supplies the book on a clear background and iOS draws the dark backdrop, or makes a one-colour version in the person's tint. Dark and tinted here are approximations.
Windows taskbar at 24 px: no box, just the book (FW Lite on Windows already ships its icon without a plate). -tiny-reversed.svg on a dark taskbar, -tiny.svg on a light one.

Do and don't

  • Pick the drawing for the size: full 96 px and up, small 48 to 95, tiny 24 to 47, micro below 24.
  • Use the reversed file on navy, dark themes and photos darker than mid-grey.
  • Keep other things at least one tab's height (a sixth of the icon's box) away from the book.
  • Use the SVG wherever it is accepted.
  • Go below 16 px.
  • Shrink the full drawing below 96 px.
  • Put the light drawing on navy or dark grounds.
  • Recolour, swap the tab colours, or add shadows, glows or gradients.
  • Straighten, rotate, stretch or crop the book. The slight turn is part of the logo.
  • Use the ล or the coral tab on their own as a logo.
  • Redraw it from a screenshot. Download the file.
