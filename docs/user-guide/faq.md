Common questions about FieldWorks Lite. Questions about syncing are answered on
[How sync works](/user-guide/how-sync-works).

### Do I need a Lexbox account?

Not to try the app. You can install FieldWorks Lite and choose **Create Example Project**
to practice on a small dictionary that stays on your device. You do need a Lexbox account
to open a real project, because that is where your team's projects live.

### Does FieldWorks Lite replace FieldWorks Classic?

No. It is a companion to FieldWorks Classic. Think of it as an easier way to do everyday
dictionary work, especially away from your desk. FieldWorks Classic is still the place
for everything else in your project.

### What can I work on in FieldWorks Lite?

The dictionary: entries, senses, definitions, examples and the pictures and sound files
that go with them. Other parts of a FieldWorks Classic project are not shown.

### Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. There are versions for Android phones and tablets and for iPhone and iPad, as well
as for Windows, Mac and Linux. It is the same app on every device.

### Is my work safe if my phone or computer breaks?

Anything that has reached Lexbox is safe. Lexbox keeps your project on the server, so you
can install FieldWorks Lite on a new device, sign in and download the project again.
Changes that are still only on a broken device cannot be recovered, so it is worth
getting online now and then.

### How do I report a problem or ask for help?

In the app, open **Feedback & Support** from the sidebar. From there you can ask a
question on the [community forum](https://community.software.sil.org/c/fwlite/48),
suggest an improvement, report a technical problem, or email
[lexbox_support@groups.sil.org](mailto:lexbox_support@groups.sil.org). Telling us what
device you were using and what you were doing at the time helps a lot.

### Is FieldWorks Lite finished?

Not yet. It is still being developed, and new things arrive regularly. Your data is
backed up on Lexbox, but if you hit a problem with a project, please say that you are
using FieldWorks Lite when you contact support.
