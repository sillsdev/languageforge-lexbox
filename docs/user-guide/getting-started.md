This page takes you from nothing installed to your first edit.

## 1. Install the app

Go to [lexbox.org/fw-lite](https://lexbox.org/fw-lite) and choose the download for your
device. On a phone or tablet, install it the same way you install any other app.

## 2. Try it first, or sign in

You do not need an account to look around. On the home screen, choose **Create Example
Project**. This makes a small practice dictionary on your device. Nothing you do in it
touches anyone else's work.

To work on a real project, choose **Login** and sign in with your Lexbox account. Your
projects on the server then appear in the list.

## 3. Download your project

Find your project in the list and choose **Download**. FieldWorks Lite copies the
dictionary onto your device. This can take a while the first time on a large project.

After that, the project stays on your device, so you can work without an internet
connection.

## 4. Open it and edit an entry

Choose **Open** on the project. Go to **Browse** to see the entries, pick one, and change
a field. To add a word, use **New Entry**.

## 5. Your changes sync on their own

You do not have to save your work to the server by hand. FieldWorks Lite sends your
changes to Lexbox and picks up other people's changes whenever it can reach the server.

See [How sync works](/user-guide/how-sync-works) for what happens to your changes, what
to expect when you are offline, and how FieldWorks Classic fits in.
