[FieldWorks Lite](/fw-lite/) lets team members do dictionary work on a phone, tablet or
computer while the rest of the team keeps using FieldWorks. This page is for the project
manager who turns it on.

## Turn it on for your project

FieldWorks Lite works with FieldWorks projects that already have data on Lexbox. A new
project stays empty until someone sends it from FieldWorks for the first time
([Fill it with data](/lexbox/host-a-project#fill-it-with-data)), so even a team that will
only use FieldWorks Lite needs FieldWorks once to start.

On your project's page on lexbox.org, choose **Try FieldWorks Lite?** and confirm with
**Yes, please!**. Lexbox prepares a FieldWorks Lite copy of your project; the first time
can take a few minutes on a large project. Afterwards the project carries a **Using
FieldWorks Lite** badge, and your team can
[install the app](https://lexbox.org/fw-lite) and
[download the project](/fw-lite/getting-started#download-your-project).

FieldWorks Lite support is still in beta, so only people with early access see the
button, and only a project manager can confirm it. Managers can request early access on
the [Where's my project?](https://lexbox.org/wheresMyProject) page.

## Keep the two sides in step

FieldWorks Lite users don't need to do anything. If some of the team still uses FieldWorks,
make this part of the team's rhythm:

1. FieldWorks users **Send/Receive** as usual.
2. An Editor or Manager presses **Sync** on the **Lexbox** tab in FieldWorks Lite (or
   **Sync FieldWorks Lite** on the project page).
3. FieldWorks users **Send/Receive** again to pick up the FieldWorks Lite work.

[How sync works](/fw-lite/how-sync-works) shows why each step is needed.
