Lexbox is the online home for your team's language projects. It stores each project on a
server where everyone's changes meet, and gives you a website,
[lexbox.org](https://lexbox.org), for managing who is on a project. Lexbox used to be
called **Language Depot**. It is the same service, and old accounts and projects are still
there.

Lexbox hosts projects for [FieldWorks](https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/) (the desktop
program, often called FLEx; FieldWorks Lite calls it *FieldWorks Classic*),
[WeSay](https://software.sil.org/wesay/),
[OneStory Editor](https://software.sil.org/onestoryeditor/), **OurWord** and **Adapt It**. Those programs exchange work with Lexbox through
**Send/Receive**. Lexbox is free to use, and is provided by SIL Language Technology and
the Linguistics Institute at Payap University.

:::tip[Do you use FieldWorks Lite?]
You rarely need this guide: syncing happens inside the app, and the
[FieldWorks Lite user guide](/fw-lite/) covers everyday work. You do need a
[Lexbox account](/lexbox/get-an-account), and your project manager adds you to the project.
:::

## In this guide

- [Get an account](/lexbox/get-an-account): register, verify your email and join your
  team's project.
- [Host a project](/lexbox/host-a-project): get a new project set up on Lexbox.
- [Send/Receive](/lexbox/send-receive/): connect FieldWorks or WeSay to your project.
- [Members and roles](/lexbox/manage-members): add people to a project and choose what
  they can do.
- [Organizations](/lexbox/organizations): group your projects and people.
- [Turn on FieldWorks Lite](/lexbox/fw-lite): for teams where some people use
  the FieldWorks Lite app.

Need help? The **Help** link on lexbox.org opens step-by-step guides, or you can write to
[lexbox_support@groups.sil.org](mailto:lexbox_support@groups.sil.org).
