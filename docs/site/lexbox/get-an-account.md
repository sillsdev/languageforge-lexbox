One Lexbox account logs you in everywhere: the lexbox.org website, Send/Receive in
FieldWorks or WeSay, and FieldWorks Lite.

## Register

Go to [lexbox.org](https://lexbox.org) and choose **Register**. You need a name, an email
address and a password. Your email address is also your Send/Receive login.
**Register with Google** fills in your name and email, but you still choose a password,
because Send/Receive needs it.

Unless you registered with Google, Lexbox then sends you a verification email. Its link
works for 3 days. Until your address is verified you can't join or request a project. Use
**Resend verification email** on the banner if it never arrived.

If you used Language Depot in the past, your account still works: log in with your email
address or your old Send/Receive login.

## Join your team's project

You don't add yourself to a project; a **project manager** adds you. Tell your manager the
email address you registered with. Once they add you, Lexbox emails you and the project
shows up under **My Projects**. Managers can also invite people who have no account yet:
the invitation email has a **Join Project** button that walks you through registering.

## Your account settings

**Account Settings** (under your name, top right) lets you change your display name, your
email address (the new address must be verified before it takes over) and the website's
display language. To change your password, use **Reset your password instead?** on that
page.

## Forgot your password?

Choose **Forgot your password?** on the login page. Lexbox emails you a link to set a new
one.
