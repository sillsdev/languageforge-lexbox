FieldWorks Lite is a fast, simple app for building a dictionary. You can use it on a
phone, a tablet or a computer. It works offline and shares your work with your team
through **Lexbox**, the online service that stores your team's projects.

FieldWorks Lite is a companion to FieldWorks Classic (the FieldWorks desktop program, also
called FLEx), not a replacement. It edits the dictionary part of a FieldWorks project; the
rest stays in FieldWorks Classic. FieldWorks Lite users get each other's changes
automatically, but changes only cross between FieldWorks Lite and FieldWorks Classic when
someone presses **Sync**. [How sync works](/fw-lite/how-sync-works) explains this.

You log in to FieldWorks Lite with a Lexbox account, but you don't need the Lexbox website
from day to day: syncing happens inside the app.

## Get FieldWorks Lite

Download it from [lexbox.org/fw-lite](https://lexbox.org/fw-lite). See
[Which devices can I use it on?](/fw-lite/faq#which-devices-can-i-use-it-on)
