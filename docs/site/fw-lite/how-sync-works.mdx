If everyone on your team uses FieldWorks Lite, there's nothing to do: your changes sync on
their own while the app is open and online. The rest of this page matters for teams where
some people also use FieldWorks Classic.

Lexbox keeps your project in **two copies**: a **FieldWorks Lite copy** and a
**FieldWorks Classic copy**.

## See it in action

Pick a question and step through what happens. Tap any colored label (**automatic**,
**Sync on Lexbox tab**, **their Send/Receive**) to see where you'll find it.

## Under the hood

    <tr>
      <td>FieldWorks Lite copy</td>
      <td>Harmony, a change database (a CRDT)</td>
      <td>Every edit is a small change record; merging is automatic and gives the same result on every device.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Step 1, "automatic"</td>
      <td>HTTP sync plus a push channel (SignalR)</td>
      <td>Runs after every edit and when you open a project. While the push channel is connected, other people's changes arrive as they happen, and a check every 5 minutes restarts a dropped channel.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Step 2, Sync on the Lexbox tab</td>
      <td>A merge job in the FwHeadless service</td>
      <td><b>There is no schedule.</b> It runs only when someone presses <i>Sync</i> on the <i>Lexbox</i> tab in FieldWorks Lite or <i>Sync FieldWorks Lite</i> on the Lexbox project page. Jobs queue one project at a time; the very first sync imports the whole project and can take minutes.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>FieldWorks Classic copy</td>
      <td>A Mercurial repository</td>
      <td>The same repository FieldWorks Classic's Send/Receive has always used.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Step 3, "Send/Receive"</td>
      <td>Chorus over Mercurial</td>
      <td>Unchanged for FieldWorks Classic users. FieldWorks Lite adds nothing they must learn.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>"The same field" clashes</td>
      <td>Deterministic merge rules</td>
      <td>Between two FieldWorks Lite users, the most recent change wins. Between FieldWorks Lite and FieldWorks Classic (since the last Sync on the Lexbox tab), the FieldWorks Classic value wins. The overwritten value stays in the word's <b>History</b>.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
