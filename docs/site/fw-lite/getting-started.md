This page takes you from nothing installed to your first edit. To open a real project,
it has to be on Lexbox, with data sent from FieldWorks Classic, and with FieldWorks Lite
turned on. If that hasn't happened yet, your project manager starts with
[Turn on FieldWorks Lite](/lexbox/fw-lite).

## Install the app

Go to [lexbox.org/fw-lite](https://lexbox.org/fw-lite) and choose the download for your
device. On an Android phone or tablet, install it from Google Play like any other app.
On a Mac, see [the early-tester version](/fw-lite/faq#which-devices-can-i-use-it-on).

## Try it first, or log in

You don't need an account to look around. On the home screen, choose **Create Example
Project**. This makes a small practice dictionary on your device. Nothing you do in it
touches anyone else's work, and it never syncs, so ignore its red sync dot.

To work on a real project, choose **Login to see projects** and log in with your
[Lexbox account](/lexbox/get-an-account). Your projects then appear under **Lexbox
Server**.

## Download your project

Find your project under **Lexbox Server** and choose **Download**. FieldWorks Lite copies
the project onto your device. On a large project this can take a while the first time.

After that, the project stays on your device, so you can work without an internet
connection.

### If your project isn't in the list

Your project shows up only when all of these are true:

- You are logged in with the account your project manager added. If **Lexbox Server**
  shows a **Login** button instead of your name, you aren't logged in yet.
- You are a member of the project. Your project manager
  [adds you](/lexbox/manage-members#add-people).
- The project is set up for FieldWorks Lite. A project manager
  [does this once on lexbox.org](/lexbox/fw-lite#turn-it-on-for-your-project).

Then press **Refresh Projects** (the circular arrow next to your name). Still missing? Choose
**I don't see my project** below the list.

## Open it and edit a word

Tap the project in the list to open it. **Browse** shows your words. Pick one and change a
field. To add a word, use **New Word** (on a phone the button just says **New**).

If you are a **Viewer** or **Commenter** on the project, it opens read-only: there is a lock
next to its name, and you can't add or change words. A Commenter can still add comments.
Ask your project manager to make you an Editor.

:::tip[Used FieldWorks Classic before?]
FieldWorks Lite says *word* and *meaning* where FieldWorks Classic says *entry* and
*sense*, and its default view hides some fields. To see more fields, with their FieldWorks
Classic names, select <AppIcon name="layers" label="view options" /> above the word
list, then under **View** choose **FieldWorks Classic**. Some FieldWorks Classic fields, such as custom fields, aren't in FieldWorks Lite yet.
:::

## Your changes sync on their own

You don't have to save your work to the server by hand. While the app is open and online,
FieldWorks Lite sends your changes to Lexbox and picks up other people's changes.

See [How sync works](/fw-lite/how-sync-works) for what happens to your changes, what to do
if someone doesn't see them, and how FieldWorks Classic fits in.
